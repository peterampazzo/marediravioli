- Impact: 7,500 DKK / 1,000 EUR donated by Oct 2024; 4,300 ravioli made and 2,600 EUR / 19,400 DKK donated by May 2026; 80 baby turtles protected Jan-Mar 2026; certificate of appreciation from MCP (Nov 2024).

YOU ALL are greatly contributing to marine conservation by buying our ravioli! How?

We're eager to tell you! We donate all the profits from our home-made ravioli to the association @marineconservationphilippines, specifically to support their 🐟marine monitoring conservation project 🐟

Through marine monitoring, MCP assesses the health of marine species and reefs, identifies actions that should be taken, and encourages them by engaging with local communities and policy-makers 🤝

Seems complex? It’s not, let us give you an example that shows why this type of research is so important: if corals are disappearing in a certain area, it might be because the algae are taking over, and that is because certain species of fish that should eat them have been overfished. Therefore, the solution lies in stricter fishing regulations that the policymakers should implement, based on the data gathered by MCP. Interesting, right? 🤩

The donations will be spent on different projects aimed to increase resilience of coastal coral reefs. This could be to strengthen enforcement of marine protected areas, enable researchers or in providing logistical support for government and monitoring teams doing crucial field work.

### 2024-07-19 (https://www.instagram.com/p/C9m8S4yoJvP/)

Choose sustainably caught or grown fish! How? Look for ASC and MSC labels 🌿🐟
There are two ways to get seafood: wild-caught or farmed. For each method, there are certifications to ensure sustainability. The Marine Stewardship Council (MSC) certifies wild-caught fish, while the Aquaculture Stewardship Council (ASC) certifies farmed fish. These labels mean the fish was produced with high environmental standards and is traceable back to a sustainable fishing operation or a responsibly run farming establishmen.
🛒 Find these labels in the supermarket for an easy sustainable choice! If a label isn't present, it might still be sustainable, but it's harder to verify, and ASC and MSC are the most common labels in Europe for seafood.
Learn more:
🔗 msc.org
🔗 asc-aqua.org
Thanks @berthevastenhoud for the precious information!
Make a difference with your choices! 🌏❤️ #SustainableSeafood #EatResponsibly
