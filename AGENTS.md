# Agent notes

One-page Vite + React + Tailwind 4 site for Mare di Ravioli. pnpm only. Run `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm test` and `pnpm build` before opening a PR; CI runs the same.

## Copy

- Facts and voice come from the Instagram captions in `docs/knowledge/instagram-captions.md`. Do not invent partners, activities or numbers that are not in there or in a newer post.
- The money goes to Marine Conservation Philippines. Say "all the profits", never "proceeds".
- Impact numbers live in `src/config/impact.config.json`, the pickup in `src/config/batch.config.json`. Update those files instead of hardcoding numbers in components.
- Keep the tone of the posts: plain, warm, first person plural, short sentences, sentence case headings. No em or en dashes, no "not X but Y" contrasts, no sales words.
